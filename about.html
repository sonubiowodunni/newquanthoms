What We Do

BANQ (New Quanthoms Billboard Agency) is a standalone marketing agency operating under Quanthom Lumina. We provide premium advertising placement through digital billboards -- image banners and video adverts displayed on our public feed.

Advertisers upload their content (image, video, or audio with an image banner) and set a destination URL. Users engage with the content and earn rewards -- Quanthom Units (QU) for clicks and Quanthom Credits (QC) for video dwell time.

Our partnership with QwkBrowser gives advertisers access to an engaged user base. BANQ operates independently with its own staff and clients, while paying QwkBrowser a commission for the relationship.

How It Works
  • Advertisers create banner campaigns via the dashboard
  • Each campaign includes an image/video, title, and destination URL
  • Banners appear on the public feed at newquanthoms.com
  • Users sign in with their QwkBrowser account to earn rewards
  • Click a banner = earn QU (10+ per click, 24h cooldown)
  • Watch a video for 3 seconds = earn QC
  • 20 unique clicks in a day = 40 QC bonus
  • Advertisers track impressions, clicks, and spend in real-time
